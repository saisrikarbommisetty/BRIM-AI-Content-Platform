FRAGRANCE: AMBRE NUIT EXTRAIT
==================================================
Brand: Maison de Parfum
Scent Family: Warm Oriental Woody Spicy
Concentration: Extrait de Parfum (25% fragrance oil concentration)

OLFACTORY PROFILE & INGREDIENT HIERARCHY:
- Top Notes (First 15 mins): Zesty Italian Bergamot, Freshly crushed Pink Pepper.
- Heart Notes (15 mins to 4 hours): Rich, velvety Turkish Rose, Spicy Indonesian Nutmeg.
- Base Notes (4 hours+): Warm Grey Ambergris (Grey Amber), Creamy Mysore Sandalwood, Sweet Madagascar Vanilla bean.

MOOD & ESSENCE:
- Evocative, mysterious, sophisticated, warm, and highly sensual.
- Designed to leave a heavy sillage (olfactory trail) and project authority.

USAGE DIRECTION:
- Best suited for evening and nighttime wear.
- Perfect for colder seasons (autumn/winter), romantic dining, and luxury formal galas.
- Apply to pulse points (wrists, side of neck, collarbone). Do not rub the fragrance.

PERFORMANCE INDEX:
- Longevity: 12+ hours on skin, 24+ hours on fabrics.
- Sillage: Heavy, memorable projection.
